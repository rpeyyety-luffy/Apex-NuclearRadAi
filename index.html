Accepted columns

  • Choose an .xlsx, .xls, or .csv file. Sensor columns named RAD-01 … RAD-05 or Sensor_1 … Sensor_5 (units such as Sensor_1_MPa are fine) are detected automatically.
  • An optional sample, time, Time_s, or timestamp column is used for the x-axis.
  • Label columns such as Anomaly_Active, Real_Leak or Faulty_Sensor are ignored.
  • Values must be numeric; blank RAD cells are treated as missing readings. At least five rows are required.

Data format and methodology

Data format

Put one reading per row. Example headers: sample, RAD-01, RAD-02, RAD-03, RAD-04, RAD-05. Values must be numeric; blank RAD cells are treated as missing readings.

How channels are assessed

  • Each channel is compared with the median of the other channels at the same moment, so a change that moves every sensor together is treated as a plant-wide event rather than a sensor fault.
  • Logistic-regression models trained on labelled recordings flag drift, dropouts, noise, stuck readings, spikes and plant-wide leaks.
  • Health scores and repair or replacement timing extrapolate the recent trend against illustrative limits; they are estimates, not instrument specifications.

Limitations

Analysis runs entirely in this browser and is a training aid, not a plant-control or safety system. A flagged channel indicates a statistical disagreement with its peers, not a confirmed radiation hazard. Results are not validated for regulatory use.
