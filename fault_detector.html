Sensor fault detector

Upload your sensor readings. Every sensor is compared with an ideal healthy run and with the other sensors, and every abnormal reading is marked on the graphs.
Ideal reference
Sensors are matched by number (RAD 1, RAD-01, Sensor 1…). A sensor whose normal level is far from the ideal is compared with its own first readings instead. Drift is recognised by a machine-learning model trained on labelled drift recordings.
